🇰🇷 Lesson 9: Directions & Transportation

🎯 What You'll Learn

Navigate Korea's excellent public transportation system, ask for directions, and understand location words. Korea's subway, bus, and KTX systems are world-class and easy to use.

Estimated Time: 45–55 minutes

✅ T-money Card (교통카드)

Get a T-money card at any convenience store — it works on all subways, buses, and even taxis and some shops. Tap on when boarding, tap off when exiting. You can recharge at convenience stores and subway machines. It's cheaper than buying single tickets!

🚕 Taking a Taxi

KoreanMeaning
[place]까지 가 주세요.Please take me to [place].
여기서 세워 주세요.Please stop here.
얼마예요?How much is it?
트렁크 열어 주세요.Please open the trunk.
카드로 해도 돼요?Can I pay by card?
💡 Culture Note: Korean taxis are safe, metered, and relatively affordable. Regular taxis (일반 택시) are silver or white; deluxe taxis (모범 택시) are black and more expensive but more comfortable. Late at night, you may need to use a taxi app like Kakao Taxi.

💬 Practice Dialogue

Finding the Way

A: 실례합니다. 명동역 어떻게 가요? (Excuse me. How do I get to Myeongdong Station?)

B: 직진해서 두 번째 사거리에서 왼쪽으로 가세요. (Go straight and turn left at the second intersection.)

A: 여기서 멀어요? (Is it far from here?)

B: 아니요, 걸어서 오 분쯤이에요. (No, it's about 5 minutes on foot.)

A: 감사합니다! (Thank you!)

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • Get a T-money card for all public transport
  • 어떻게 가요? = "How do I get there?" — most useful direction question
  • 까지 가 주세요 = "Please take me to..." for taxis
  • Seoul subway lines are numbered and color-coded — very foreigner-friendly
  • Key locations: 역 (station), 출구 (exit), 정류장 (bus stop)
