💫 Understanding 정 (Jeong)

정 is one of the most important cultural concepts in Korea, and there's no exact English translation. It's the deep emotional attachment and affection that builds between people over time — a bond of loyalty, warmth, and caring that goes beyond simple friendship or love.

정 applies to family, friends, coworkers, even your favorite restaurant owner. Koreans say "정이 들다" (jeong settles in) when you've developed this bond. It's why Koreans share food from the same dish, why leaving a job feels so emotional, and why your Korean friend's grandmother will feed you like you're her own grandchild.

💡 파이팅 (Fighting!): This English loanword is one of the most common expressions of encouragement in Korea. Koreans use it before exams, sports games, job interviews — any challenging situation. Say it with a fist pump! Sometimes spelled 화이팅.

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • 기분이 좋아요/안 좋아요 = "I feel good/bad" — most versatile mood expression
  • 정 (jeong) = the deep emotional bond unique to Korean culture
  • 파이팅! = universal encouragement phrase
  • 보고 싶어요 = "I miss you" — commonly used with friends and loved ones
  • 선배/후배 hierarchy shapes all Korean relationships
