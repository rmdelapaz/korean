🇰🇷 Lesson 14: Weather & Seasons

🎯 What You'll Learn

Discuss weather, Korea's four distinct seasons, and seasonal activities. Korea has dramatic seasonal changes — from cherry blossoms in spring to heavy monsoons in summer to vibrant autumn foliage.

💡 장마 (Jangma) — Monsoon Season

Korea's monsoon season (장마) usually runs from late June to mid-July. Expect heavy rain, humidity, and occasional flooding. Koreans stock up on rain gear and enjoy indoor activities. Summer also brings 삼복 (the three hottest days), when people eat 삼계탕 (ginseng chicken soup) to fight heat with heat — a traditional Korean approach!

⚠️ 미세먼지 (Fine Dust)

Fine dust pollution (미세먼지) is a major concern in Korea, especially in spring. Koreans check air quality apps daily and wear masks (마스크) on bad days. Check the 미세먼지 forecast before planning outdoor activities! The phrase 미세먼지가 심해요 (the fine dust is bad) is very commonly used.

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • Korea has four distinct seasons with dramatic changes
  • 날씨가 어때요? = "How's the weather?"
  • 장마 (monsoon) in June-July brings heavy rain
  • 미세먼지 (fine dust) is a daily concern — check the app!
  • Each season has beloved foods and activities
