🇰🇷 Lesson 8: Shopping & Money

🎯 What You'll Learn

Shop confidently in Korea — from traditional markets to department stores. Learn about Korean won, prices, bargaining, and essential shopping phrases.

Estimated Time: 40–50 minutes

💰 Korean Won (원)

Korean currency is the won (₩). There are no decimals — prices are in whole numbers. Common denominations: ₩1,000 · ₩5,000 · ₩10,000 · ₩50,000 bills and ₩10 · ₩50 · ₩100 · ₩500 coins.

💡 Culture Note: Bargaining (깎아 주세요) is common at traditional markets (시장) but NOT at department stores, convenience stores, or chain shops. Korea is very card-friendly — you can pay by card almost everywhere, even for small amounts.

💬 Practice Dialogue

At Namdaemun Market

Customer: 이거 얼마예요? (How much is this?)

Vendor: 만 오천 원이에요. (15,000 won.)

Customer: 너무 비싸요. 만 원에 안 돼요? (Too expensive. Can't you do 10,000 won?)

Vendor: 만 이천 원에 해 드릴게요. (I'll give it to you for 12,000 won.)

Customer: 좋아요. 카드 돼요? (Okay. Do you take card?)

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • 이거 얼마예요? = How much is this? (most useful shopping phrase)
  • Korean won uses Sino-Korean numbers: 천 원, 만 원, etc.
  • Bargain at 시장 (markets) with 깎아 주세요, but not at chain stores
  • Korea is extremely card-friendly — 카드 돼요? to ask
  • Size words are often English loanwords (스몰, 미디엄, 라지)
