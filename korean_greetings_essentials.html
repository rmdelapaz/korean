🇰🇷 Lesson 4: Greetings & Essential Phrases

🎯 What You'll Learn

Learn how to greet people, introduce yourself, and use essential polite expressions. You'll also understand Korea's speech levels (존댓말 vs 반말) and when to use each.

Estimated Time: 45–55 minutes

📖 Speech Levels in Korean

Korean has multiple speech levels based on formality and politeness. As a beginner, focus on two:

LevelNameWhen to UseEnding
Formal Polite합쇼체 (hapsyo-che)Business, presentations, strangers, elders-습니다 / -ㅂ니다
Informal Polite해요체 (haeyo-che)Everyday conversations, most situations-아요 / -어요

⚠️ Important: 존댓말 (Polite Speech)

Always use polite speech (존댓말) with people you've just met, people older than you, or in formal situations. Using casual speech (반말) with the wrong person is considered very rude in Korean culture. When in doubt, go formal!

👋 Basic Greetings

KoreanRomanizationMeaningWhen to Use

안녕히 가세요annyeonghi gaseyoGoodbye (to the person leaving)You're staying, they're going
안녕히 계세요annyeonghi gyeseyoGoodbye (to the person staying)You're leaving, they're staying

✅ Two Ways to Say Goodbye!

Korean has two goodbyes depending on who's leaving. If you're both leaving, either works, but 안녕히 가세요 is most common.

🤝 Self-Introduction (자기소개)

Here's a standard self-introduction pattern you can customize:

안녕하세요. (Hello.)

저는 [이름]입니다. (I am [name].)

미국 사람입니다. (I am American.)

만나서 반갑습니다. (Nice to meet you.)

잘 부탁합니다. (Please take care of me. / I look forward to working with you.)

💬 Practice Dialogue

Meeting Someone New

A: 안녕하세요! (Hello!)

B: 안녕하세요! (Hello!)

A: 저는 레이입니다. 이름이 뭐예요? (I'm Ray. What's your name?)

B: 저는 수진이에요. 만나서 반가워요! (I'm Sujin. Nice to meet you!)

A: 어디에서 왔어요? (Where are you from?)

B: 한국에서 왔어요. 레이 씨는요? (I'm from Korea. How about you, Ray?)

A: 미국에서 왔어요. (I'm from America.)

💡 Culture Note — 씨 (ssi): 씨 is an honorific like "Mr./Ms." attached to a person's full name or first name. Never use 씨 with just a last name — that's rude. Say "수진 씨" or "김수진 씨", not "김 씨."

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • Use 존댓말 (polite speech) with strangers, elders, and in formal settings
  • 안녕하세요 works as "hello" in any polite situation
  • There are two goodbyes: 가세요 (to the leaver) and 계세요 (to the stayer)
  • Self-introductions use 저는 [이름]입니다
  • Add 씨 after first names or full names as an honorific
  • Nationality = country name + 사람 (person)
