A complete A1–A2 Korean course — from your first Hangul letter to TOPIK I

🌟 What This Course Covers

Twenty-eight lessons take you from reading Hangul to the end of A2. You will be able to handle everyday situations, describe your life, ask and answer questions, talk about the past and the future, and choose the right level of politeness for the person in front of you. Topic lessons give you the words; the 📐 Grammar Essentials lessons woven between them give you the machinery — particles and verb endings — to build your own sentences instead of reciting phrases.

Which level is this? Korean is measured on its own scale, the TOPIK (Test of Proficiency in Korean). Lessons 1–15 cover TOPIK level 1 (≈ CEFR A1) and lessons 16–28 cover TOPIK level 2 (≈ CEFR A2), following the grade 1–2 content of the National Institute of Korean Language's international standard curriculum. Finish the course and you are ready to sit TOPIK I.

Built in: 🔊 audio on every Korean word · a spaced-repetition review deck · flashcards · self-check quizzes · written exercises that check your answers · a learning journal · progress tracking.
