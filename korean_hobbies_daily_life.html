🇰🇷 Lesson 11: Hobbies & Daily Life

🎯 What You'll Learn

Talk about hobbies, daily routines, K-pop, K-dramas, and Korean leisure culture including PC방 (PC cafes) and 노래방 (karaoke rooms).

Estimated Time: 45–55 minutes

📝 Grammar: ~는 것을 좋아해요 (I like doing...)

To say you like doing something: Verb stem + 는 것을 좋아해요

KoreanMeaning
음악 듣는 것을 좋아해요.I like listening to music.
한국 드라마 보는 것을 좋아해요.I like watching Korean dramas.
요리하는 것을 좋아해요.I like cooking.

Simpler version: 취미가 뭐예요? (What's your hobby?) → 제 취미는 [hobby]이에요/예요.

💬 Practice Dialogue

A: 취미가 뭐예요? (What's your hobby?)

B: 저는 K-pop 듣는 것을 좋아해요. BTS 팬이에요! (I like listening to K-pop. I'm a BTS fan!)

A: 대박! 저도요! 주말에 뭐 해요? (Amazing! Me too! What do you do on weekends?)

B: 보통 카페에서 독서하거나 한강에서 산책해요. (Usually I read at a cafe or take walks at the Han River.)

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • 취미가 뭐예요? = "What's your hobby?"
  • ~는 것을 좋아해요 = "I like doing..." pattern
  • Korean leisure: 노래방 (karaoke), PC방 (gaming), 찜질방 (spa), 치맥 (chicken+beer)
  • K-pop/K-drama vocabulary is useful for connecting with Koreans
