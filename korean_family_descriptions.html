🇰🇷 Lesson 6: Family & Personal Descriptions

🎯 What You'll Learn

Korean has specific family terms based on whether the person is from the mother's or father's side, and whether you're male or female. You'll also learn to describe people using adjectives and the topic/subject markers.

Estimated Time: 45–55 minutes

⚠️ Sibling Terms Depend on YOUR Gender

A male calls his older brother 형 and older sister 누나. A female calls her older brother 오빠 and older sister 언니. These are also used with close non-family members (friends, K-pop fans calling idols 오빠/언니).

📝 Grammar: Topic Marker 은/는 & Subject Marker 이/가

MarkerAfter ConsonantAfter VowelFunction
Topic은 (eun)는 (neun)Marks the topic ("as for X...")
Subject이 (i)가 (ga)Marks the grammatical subject

저는 학생이에요. = As for me, I'm a student. (Topic: me)

날씨가 좋아요. = The weather is nice. (Subject: weather — new info)

💬 Practice Dialogue

Talking About Family

A: 가족이 몇 명이에요? (How many people are in your family?)

B: 네 명이에요. 아버지, 어머니, 언니, 그리고 저예요. (Four. My father, mother, older sister, and me.)

A: 언니는 뭐 해요? (What does your older sister do?)

B: 언니는 선생님이에요. 아주 친절해요. (She's a teacher. She's very kind.)

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • Family terms vary by your gender and the relative's age/side
  • 은/는 = topic marker ("as for..."), 이/가 = subject marker (new info)
  • Korean adjectives conjugate like verbs
  • 오빠/언니/형/누나 are also used for close non-family members
