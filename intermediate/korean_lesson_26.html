🇰🇷 Lesson 26: TOPIK II Strategy (3–4급)

Three hours, one hundred and four questions, and a single number at the end. For many learners TOPIK II is the moment when months of study turn into something a university, an employer or an immigration office can read. The good news: you already know the Korean this exam tests. Reported speech, -더-, passives, 한다체, headlines, graphs, opinion essays — every one of those lessons was quietly TOPIK training. What this capstone adds is the exam craft: how the paper is built, what each question type is really asking, where the points are, and how to plan your time before and during the test.

⚠️ Check the Official Details

This lesson describes the paper-based TOPIK II as it has run for years. Formats, timings and rules do change — and there is now an internet-based test (TOPIK IBT) in some countries, with its own format, plus a separate speaking test. Before you register, confirm the current format, dates and rules on the official TOPIK website, topik.go.kr.

🧠 The Mental Model: One Exam, Four Levels

TOPIK II is a single exam that awards level 3, 4, 5 or 6 depending on your total score. There is no separate "level 3 test": everyone sits the same paper, and the easier early questions are exactly where level-3 candidates earn most of their points. That changes how you should think about it — you are not trying to answer everything, you are trying to collect enough points.

💡 What the Numbers Mean

Level 3 needs 40% of the total; level 4 needs 50%. You can miss half the paper and still get 4급. That's why strategy matters: every minute spent agonising over the final, hardest reading passage is a minute not spent securing easier points.

🎧 듣기: The Listening Section

The fifty listening questions run roughly from easy to hard. The exact numbering shifts from year to year, but the shape is stable:

StageTypical taskWhat it tests
Opening questionsChoose the picture or graph that matches a short dialogue; choose the next line; choose what the woman will do nextEveryday situations, reported information, 해요체 dialogue
MiddleChoose the statement that matches the content; identify the speaker's main ideaWorkplace and public-facility talk (Lessons 13–14), inference (Lesson 4)
Later (paired questions)Two questions per recording: interviews, lectures, documentaries, debates — the speaker's attitude, purpose, main claimFormal speech, 하십시오체, Sino-Korean vocabulary, argument structure

✅ Listening Tactics

Read ahead. Use every pause — including the instructions — to read the next question and its options. Knowing that the question asks "what will the man do next?" tells you what to listen for.

Listen for the turn. The answer often comes after 그런데, 하지만, 그래서 or 그러니까 — the moment the speaker shifts from background to point.

Main-idea questions want the speaker's opinion, not a fact they mentioned. Listen for -아야 한다, -는 것이 중요하다, -(으)면 좋겠다 (Lesson 22).

Never chase a missed question. You cannot rewind. Mark your best guess immediately and get ready for the next recording.

51 & 52: Match the Register, Follow the Logic

Both are about reading the text around the blank. Ask two questions: what does the logic demand (a reason? a contrast? a request?) and what register is the text in? A notice or email in 51 usually uses -습니다/-(으)ㅂ니다 or polite 해요체, and your sentence must match it. The paragraph in 52 is in 한다체, so your sentence must be too — a single -요 in 52 costs points.

53: Describing a Graph (200–300 characters)

Question 53 is the most formulaic part of the exam — which makes it the best return on your study time. The structure is almost always the same: who was surveyed → the main results → a change or comparison → reasons or outlook, all in 한다체. Learn this phrase bank until it is automatic:

FunctionKoreanEnglish
Introduce the surveyN을/를 대상으로 N에 대해 조사하였다.A survey was conducted of N on N.
Report results조사 결과, N이/가 N%로 가장 높게 나타났다.According to the results, N was highest at N%.
Rank the rest그 뒤를 이어 N(으)로 나타났다.… followed, at N.
Change over timeN년 N에서 N년 N(으)로 증가하였다 / 감소하였다.Rose / fell from N in (year) to N in (year).
MultiplesN년 사이에 두 배 이상 늘었다.More than doubled in N years.
Reasons이러한 변화의 원인으로는 N을/를 들 수 있다.Causes of this change include N.
Outlook이러한 추세는 앞으로도 계속될 것으로 예상된다.This trend is expected to continue.

Sample prompt (fictional data): a graph shows how 300 office workers in their twenties spend their time after work — exercise 35%, watching videos 30%, self-improvement 20%, other 15% — and a second chart shows self-improvement rising from 10% in 2015 to 20% in 2025, with two reasons given (changing views of lifetime employment; more online courses).

Model answer (about 230 characters)
한국연구소에서 20대 직장인 300명을 대상으로 퇴근 후 여가 활동에 대해 조사하였다. 조사 결과 '운동'이 35%로 가장 높게 나타났으며, '동영상 시청'이 30%, '자기계발'이 20%로 그 뒤를 이었다. 특히 자기계발을 한다는 응답은 2015년 10%에서 2025년 20%로 10년 사이에 두 배 증가하였다. 이러한 변화의 원인으로는 평생직장에 대한 인식 변화와 온라인 강의의 증가를 들 수 있다. 앞으로도 이러한 추세는 계속될 것으로 예상된다.
English translation

The Korea Institute surveyed 300 office workers in their twenties about their leisure activities after work. The survey showed "exercise" highest at 35%, followed by "watching videos" at 30% and "self-improvement" at 20%. In particular, responses of doing self-improvement doubled in ten years, from 10% in 2015 to 20% in 2025. Causes of this change include a shift in attitudes towards lifetime employment and the growth of online courses. This trend is expected to continue.

⚠️ Question 53 Traps

Don't give your opinion. 53 is a description task. 저는 운동이 좋다고 생각한다 has no place here.

Don't copy the prompt's title word for word as your whole first sentence — rephrase it into the survey frame.

Use every piece of information the prompt shows. If it gives two reasons, both must appear.

54: The Opinion Essay (600–700 characters)

You met this in Lesson 22. The prompt usually introduces a social issue and asks two or three sub-questions (why is X important? what problems arise? how can we solve them?). Examiners reward answering every sub-question in order, clear paragraphs and 한다체 with B2 connectives.

💡 원고지 (Manuscript Paper) Basics

Answers to 53 and 54 are written on squared manuscript paper. Each Hangul syllable, each space and each punctuation mark takes one square; start each new paragraph by leaving the first square blank. Practise on printed 원고지 at least a few times — counting characters is much easier when you can see the squares, and answer sheets typically mark every hundred squares, so you can see at a glance when you have passed 600.

📖 읽기: The Reading Section

Fifty questions in seventy minutes is about 84 seconds per question — but the early questions take twenty seconds and the late ones take three minutes. Again, the shape is easy to hard:

StageTypical taskLesson link
OpeningChoose the grammar that fits a blank; choose an expression with a similar meaning; what is this ad or notice about?The whole grammar arc (Lessons 2–20)
Early–middleMatch a graph or notice to a statement; put sentences in order; infer a blankConnectives (Lesson 22), 53-style language
MiddleInterpret a news headline; choose the main ideaHeadline style (Lesson 21), idioms (Lesson 24)
LaterWhere does this sentence fit? Longer editorials, essays and literary excerpts: the writer's attitude, a character's feeling, the purpose of the text한다체, Sino-Korean roots (Lesson 23), -더- (Lesson 17)

✅ Reading Tactics

Question first, passage second. Know what you're hunting for before you read.

Grammar blanks: test each option. Many distractors are grammatically possible but break a restriction you've learned — -느라고 with a positive result, -아서 before a command, -더- with a first-person feeling.

Main-idea questions: the answer usually paraphrases the last sentence or the sentence after 그러므로/따라서/즉. An option that is merely true but not central is the classic trap.

Ordering questions: find the sentence that can't be first (it starts with 그래서, 이것은, 그 결과) and chain from pronouns and connectives.

Budget your time. Aim to reach the halfway point with well over half your time left.

Korean

English translation

Ahead of exams, many test-takers stay up all night studying. However, experts warn that cutting back on sleep can actually harm your score. That is because our brains organise and store what we learned during the day while we sleep. So if you don't sleep enough, you easily forget even what you studied hard. Also, when you are short of sleep your concentration drops, making it easy to make mistakes during the exam. On the day before an exam, rather than studying new material, it is better to lightly review what you have already studied and go to bed early.

This is a textbook TOPIK passage: a common belief, then 그러나, the expert view, reasons with -기 때문이다, 따라서, and a recommendation at the end. The last sentence is the main idea — exactly what a 중심 생각 question would ask for.

🌏 Cultural note: In Korea, exam season comes with rituals. People give test-takers 엿 (sticky taffy) and 찹쌀떡 (sticky rice cakes) so the answers will "stick" — 붙다 means both "to stick" and "to pass" — and, as you learned in Lesson 24, they avoid 미역국 before the exam. You may find a friend handing you some 엿 before your TOPIK day.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

⚠️ Watch Out For

해요체 in 52, 53 or 54. Written answers must be in 한다체 (중요하다, 늘고 있다, 것이다). Mixing in -요 is one of the most common and costly errors.

Spending 40 minutes on 51–53. 54 is worth half the writing score. Keep 25 minutes for it no matter what.

Leaving 54 blank. An unfinished but organised essay still earns points; a blank one earns none. If time is short, write the introduction and one body paragraph.

Spoken forms in writing. 근데, 너무 (in the neutral "very" sense), 되게, -거든요 and 해 버렸다 read as casual. Use 그런데/그러나, 매우, 아주.

Leaving multiple-choice answers empty. There's no penalty for guessing wrong on the paper-based test, so never leave a bubble blank.

Studying only what's comfortable. Doing reading drills because you like reading won't move the score if writing is your weakest section.

Exercise 1 — 51-style blanks: fit the logic and the register

❓ Common Questions

Should I aim for level 3 or level 4?

If you have worked through all 26 lessons of this tier, aim for 4급 — the whole tier was built around it. If you finished only the B1 lessons (1–14) or your mock tests are below 110, target 3급 first. Many learners take TOPIK II twice: once to get level 3 and learn the exam, again for level 4.

Paper test or internet-based test (IBT)?

That depends on what is offered where you live and what the institution you're applying to accepts. The IBT is taken on a computer and has its own format and question counts, so if you choose it, practise with its official sample materials. Check both options on topik.go.kr.

What if my handwriting is bad?

Examiners need to read your answer, so write clearly and at a steady size in the squares. Neatness matters more than beauty. Practise on real 원고지 so you get used to one syllable per square.

How long is a TOPIK score valid?

Scores are generally treated as valid for two years from the results date, but the institution receiving your score may have its own rules — check with them and with the official site.

Challenge 1: Download one past TOPIK II paper from the official site and sit it under real timing. Score it, then write down the three question types that cost you the most points — that's your study plan.

Mock scores go up in steps, not in a smooth line — it's completely normal to stay flat for a few weeks and then jump ten points in one test. A disappointing practice score isn't a verdict on your Korean; it's a map showing exactly where the next points are hiding.
