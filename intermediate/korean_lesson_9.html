🇰🇷 Lesson 9: Purpose, Intention & Decisions

Every January, and then again at 설날 (the Lunar New Year), Korea fills up with plans. Gyms sell year-long memberships, bookshops put TOPIK prep books at the front, and everyone asks each other: 새해 목표가 뭐예요? ("What's your New Year's goal?"). To answer well, you need to say what you want to achieve, why you're doing something, so that something else can happen, and how firmly you've decided — "I'm thinking of…", "I've decided to…", "I was just about to!"

At A2 you learned -(으)러 ("in order to go and…") and -(으)려고 하다 ("intend to"). This lesson builds the rest of the toolkit, and it spends real time on a set of endings that all translate as "so that" or "in order to" in English but are not interchangeable in Korean: -기 위해서, -도록, -게.

🧠 The Mental Model: Aims and Certainty

This lesson has two halves.

HalfQuestion it answersPatterns
PurposeWhy? For what? So that what happens?-기 위해(서), N을/를 위해(서), -도록, -게, -고자 (formal), review: -(으)려고, -(으)러
Intention & decisionHow firmly am I planning this?-(으)ㄹ까 하다, -(으)려고 하다, -기로 하다, -(으)려던 참이다

For the purpose half, the key question is: whose goal is it, and is it an action or a result? If the subject is deliberately aiming at their own action, it's -기 위해서 territory. If you're doing something to bring about a state or to let someone else do something, it's -도록/-게 territory. Keep that question in your pocket.

🎯 -기 위해(서): "In Order To"

위하다 means "to serve, to be for the sake of". -기 위해서 turns a verb into a goal: "for the purpose of doing". 서 is optional: -기 위해 sounds a little more written.

💡 The Formula

Verb stem + -기 위해(서) → 합격하기 위해서, 살을 빼기 위해서, 돈을 모으기 위해서.

Noun + 을/를 위해(서) → 가족을 위해서, 건강을 위해서, 미래를 위해.

Before a noun: -기 위한 N / N을 위한 N → 합격하기 위한 전략 ("a strategy for passing"), 외국인을 위한 안내 ("information for foreigners").

KoreanEnglish
한국 회사에 취직하기 위해서 한국어를 공부하고 있어요.I'm studying Korean in order to get a job at a Korean company.
비행기표를 싸게 사기 위해 새벽까지 기다렸어요.I waited up till dawn to buy a cheap plane ticket.
부모님은 저를 위해서 많은 것을 포기하셨어요.My parents gave up a lot for me.
이건 너를 위한 선물이야.This is a present for you.
건강해지기 위해서 매일 만 보씩 걸어요.I walk ten thousand steps a day to get healthier.

⚠️ Two Restrictions on -기 위해서

1. Verbs only, not adjectives. A goal has to be something you do. ✗ 건강하기 위해서 → ✓ 건강해지기 위해서 ("to become healthy") or ✓ 건강을 위해서 ("for my health").

2. Same subject. The person with the goal is the person doing the main action. ✗ 아이가 잘 자기 위해서 제가 불을 껐어요 (the child's goal? my action?) → ✓ 아이가 잘 자도록 제가 불을 껐어요. That's the job of -도록, next.

Review: -기 위해서 vs -(으)려고 vs -(으)러

You already know two other "in order to" endings. Here is how they divide the work:

-(으)러-(으)려고-기 위해서
Main verbonly movement verbs (가다, 오다, 다니다…)any verbany verb
Command / suggestion after it✓ 밥 먹으러 가자.✗✓
Feeleverydayeveryday, personal intentionneutral to formal, a bigger "goal"
Example책을 빌리러 도서관에 가요.살을 빼려고 운동해요.살을 빼기 위해서 운동해요.

The command restriction on -(으)려고 is worth remembering: ✗ 살을 빼려고 운동하세요 → ✓ 살을 빼기 위해서 운동하세요 / 살을 빼고 싶으면 운동하세요.

🎈 -도록: "So That" (and "Until")

-도록 attaches straight to the stem: 보이도록, 자도록, 늦지 않도록, 이해할 수 있도록. Its core meaning is "to the point where X is the case". From that come three uses.

Use 1: "So that" — aiming at a result

Here -도록 describes the state or outcome you want to bring about. That outcome can involve a different person, or be a state rather than a deliberate action — exactly the cases where -기 위해서 fails or sounds stiff.

KoreanEnglish
감기에 걸리지 않도록 손을 자주 씻으세요.Wash your hands often so that you don't catch a cold.
학생들이 쉽게 이해할 수 있도록 예를 많이 들었어요.I gave lots of examples so the students could understand easily.
아이가 잘 자도록 불을 꺼 주세요.Please turn off the light so the child can sleep well.
잊어버리지 않도록 휴대폰에 메모해 두세요.Make a note on your phone so you don't forget.

Notice how often -도록 comes with -지 않도록 ("so as not to") and -(으)ㄹ 수 있도록 ("so that … can"). These two combinations cover a huge share of real use. (You will also see -지 않기 위해서; it isn't wrong, but for preventing something, -지 않도록 is the more natural choice.)

Use 2: "Until / to the point that" — degree and time

KoreanEnglish
친구하고 밤새도록 이야기했어요.I talked with my friend all night long (until the night was over).
목이 쉬도록 응원했어요.We cheered until we were hoarse.
밤늦도록 사무실에 불이 켜져 있었어요.The office lights were on until late at night.

This is the -도록 you met at the end of Lesson 8: like -(으)ㄹ 때까지, it marks a limit, but it adds a sense of "to that extent". 밤새도록 is so common it's practically a single word.

Use 3: -도록 하다 — polite instructions and resolutions

In the second person, -도록 하세요 is a softened, slightly formal instruction ("be sure to…"), much loved by doctors, teachers and bosses. In the first person, -도록 하겠습니다 is a promise or resolution.

KoreanEnglish
내일까지 보고서를 제출하도록 하세요.Please make sure to submit the report by tomorrow.
당분간 매운 음식은 드시지 않도록 하세요.Please avoid spicy food for the time being.
다음부터는 늦지 않도록 하겠습니다.I'll make sure not to be late from now on.

🎨 -게: "So That" (Conversational)

You know -게 as the ending that turns adjectives into adverbs: 예쁘다 → 예쁘게, 빠르다 → 빠르게. Stretch that idea a little and -게 can describe the result you want: "(do it) in a way that makes X happen" = "so that X". In this use it overlaps with -도록, but it is lighter and more conversational.

KoreanEnglish
뒤에 앉은 사람도 잘 보이게 크게 써 주세요.Please write it big so the people at the back can see it too.
아이가 깨지 않게 조용히 말해.Talk quietly so the baby doesn't wake up.
먹기 좋게 잘라 드릴까요?Shall I cut it up so it's easy to eat?
방이 따뜻하게 창문 좀 닫아 줄래?Could you close the window so the room stays warm?

Mostly -게 and -도록 can swap in the "so that" use: 잘 보이게 / 잘 보이도록. The difference is register — -게 for chatting, -도록 for instructions, notices and writing. The "until / to the point that" meaning (Use 2 above) belongs mainly to -도록; -게 does it only in a few fixed expressions like 배 터지게 먹었어요 ("I ate till I nearly burst"). (Don't confuse this with -게 하다, "to make someone do", which is a causative — Lesson 16.)

💡 One Situation, Three Sentences

시험에 합격하기 위해서 매일 공부해요 — I study every day to pass the exam. (My goal, my action.)

학생들이 합격할 수 있도록 선생님이 매일 도와주세요 — The teacher helps every day so that the students can pass. (Different subjects: -도록.)

잘 외워지게 소리 내서 읽어 봐 — Read it out loud so it sticks. (Result state, casual: -게.)

🎩 -고자: Formal Purpose and Intention

-고자 is the formal, written relative of -(으)려고. You'll hear it in speeches, news, presentations and official emails, never in chat with friends. As a connector it means "in order to"; as -고자 하다 it means "intend to" (= formal -(으)려고 하다). Same subject throughout.

KoreanEnglish
오늘은 새로운 제품을 소개하고자 합니다.Today I would like to introduce our new product.
더 나은 서비스를 제공하고자 노력하고 있습니다.We are working hard to provide better service.
이 자리를 빌려 감사의 말씀을 드리고자 합니다.I'd like to take this opportunity to express my thanks.

📏 How Sure Am I? The Intention Scale

Now the second half. Korean has a neat scale from "toying with the idea" to "it's settled".

CertaintyPatternExampleEnglish
Low — just an idea-(으)ㄹ까 하다주말에 영화나 볼까 해요.I'm thinking I might see a film this weekend.
Medium — intention-(으)려고 하다주말에 영화를 보려고 해요.I'm planning to see a film this weekend.
High — decided / arranged-기로 하다주말에 영화를 보기로 했어요.I've decided (arranged) to see a film this weekend.

-(으)ㄹ까 하다: "I'm thinking of…"

Literally "I say to myself, 'shall I…?'". It's tentative and first-person: you use it for your own half-formed plans, often with 나 ("or something") to soften further. Past -(으)ㄹ까 했어요 usually implies the plan didn't happen.

KoreanEnglish
올해는 수영을 배울까 해요.I'm thinking of learning to swim this year.
머리를 좀 짧게 자를까 하는데 어때요?I'm thinking of cutting my hair a bit shorter — what do you think?
오늘 저녁은 그냥 라면이나 끓여 먹을까 해.I think I'll just make some ramyeon tonight.
등산을 갈까 했는데 비가 와서 못 갔어요.I was thinking of going hiking, but it rained so I couldn't.

-기로 하다: "Decide / Agree To"

-기로 하다 marks a decision, either your own or one made with someone else (an arrangement). It's almost always in the past (-기로 했어요), because the deciding already happened. Stronger verbs can replace 하다: -기로 결심하다 ("resolve to"), -기로 결정하다 ("decide officially"), -기로 약속하다 ("promise to").

KoreanEnglish
올해는 TOPIK 4급을 따기로 했어요.I've decided to get TOPIK level 4 this year.
친구랑 토요일에 만나기로 했어.I've arranged to meet my friend on Saturday.
건강 때문에 담배를 끊기로 결심했어요.I resolved to quit smoking for my health.
회사에서 재택근무를 늘리기로 결정했습니다.The company has decided to expand remote work.
우리 앞으로 매일 한국어로만 이야기하기로 해요.Let's agree to speak only Korean from now on.

Negatives: 안 가기로 했어요 or 가지 않기로 했어요 ("decided not to go"). The last example shows the propositive: -기로 해요 = "let's make it a rule to…".

-(으)려던 참이다: "I Was Just About To"

참 means "the very moment". -(으)려던 참이다 says you were right on the point of doing something — typically when someone suggests or asks about exactly that thing. Form: 가려던, 먹으려던, 만들려던 (ㄹ-stems take no 으). The past -(으)려던 참이었어요 is the most common form; the present -(으)려던 참이에요 is also used when the moment is still now. Adding 마침 ("just then") or 막 ("just") is very natural.

SpeakerKoreanEnglish
가커피 한잔할래요?Fancy a coffee?
나좋아요. 안 그래도 마시려던 참이었어요.Sure. I was just about to have one anyway.
가민수 씨한테 연락해 봤어?Have you tried getting in touch with Min-su?
나마침 지금 전화하려던 참이야.I'm just about to call him now, actually.
가나가려던 참에 비가 오기 시작했어요.Just as I was about to go out, it started to rain.

라이언하하, 좋아요. 사실 저도 그 부탁을 하려던 참이었어요.Ha ha, great. Actually, I was just about to ask you for that.
하은그럼 우리 서로 도와주기로 해요. 작심삼일 금지!Then let's agree to help each other. No three-day resolutions!

Look at who the goals belong to. 합격하기 위해서 and 건강해지기 위해서 are each speaker's own aims. 늦잠 자지 않도록 and 포기하지 않게 are about the other person — so they need -도록/-게.

📅 Cultural note — 작심삼일 and two New Years. 작심삼일 (作心三日, "a resolve that lasts three days") is the idiom every Korean reaches for when a resolution collapses; you'll meet more four-character idioms like it in Lesson 24. The good news: Korea celebrates both the solar New Year (1월 1일) and 설날, the Lunar New Year, a few weeks later. People joke that if their January resolutions fail, 설날 is the official second chance.

📰 Reading: 새해 목표, 끝까지 지키는 법

A short advice column from a lifestyle magazine, written in friendly 해요체.

Korean
새해가 되면 많은 사람들이 목표를 세워요. 하지만 2월이 되기도 전에 포기하는 사람이 적지 않아요. 목표를 끝까지 지키기 위해서는 무엇이 필요할까요? 첫째, 목표는 구체적이어야 해요. "운동을 열심히 하기로 했다"보다 "일주일에 세 번 30분씩 걷기로 했다"가 훨씬 좋아요. 둘째, 잊어버리지 않도록 목표를 종이에 써서 잘 보이는 곳에 붙이세요. 셋째, 가족이나 친구에게 목표를 말하세요. 다른 사람이 알고 있으면 쉽게 포기할 수 없거든요. 마지막으로, 하루쯤 실패해도 괜찮아요. 중요한 것은 다음 날 다시 시작하는 거예요. 올해는 여러분도 작은 목표부터 시작해 볼까요?
English translation

When the new year comes, lots of people set goals. But quite a few give up before February even arrives. What do you need in order to keep your goals to the end? First, a goal has to be specific. "I've decided to walk for 30 minutes three times a week" is much better than "I've decided to exercise hard". Second, write your goal on a piece of paper and stick it somewhere you can see it, so that you don't forget. Third, tell your family or friends about your goal. When other people know, you can't give up so easily. Finally, it's fine to fail for a day or so. What matters is starting again the next day. How about you start with a small goal this year too?

⚠️ Common Mistakes

1. Adjectives with -기 위해서. ✗ 예쁘기 위해서 → ✓ 예뻐지기 위해서.

2. Different subjects with -기 위해서. ✗ 손님들이 편하게 쉬기 위해서 우리가 방을 청소했어요. → ✓ 손님들이 편하게 쉴 수 있도록 우리가 방을 청소했어요.

3. -(으)려고 before a command. ✗ 합격하려고 열심히 공부하세요. → ✓ 합격하기 위해서 / 합격할 수 있도록 열심히 공부하세요.

4. -기로 하다 in the present for a decision. ✗ 저는 내년에 유학을 가기로 해요 (sounds like "let's decide") → ✓ 가기로 했어요.

5. -(으)ㄹ까 하다 about other people. ✗ 민수 씨가 이사할까 해요. → ✓ 민수 씨가 이사할까 한대요 (reported, Lesson 3) or 이사하려고 한대요. The tentative "shall I?" belongs to the speaker.

6. Extra 으 on ㄹ-stems. ✗ 만들으려던 참이에요 → ✓ 만들려던 참이에요.

  8. 오늘은 새로운 제품을 _____ 합니다. (소개하다, formal intention) (Answer: 소개하고자)

Exercise 4 — Fix the purpose ending

  1. ✗ 살을 빼려고 운동하세요. → 살을 _____ 운동하세요. (빼다, -기 위해서) (Answer: 빼기 위해서|빼기 위해)
  2. ✗ 아이가 잘 자기 위해서 제가 불을 껐어요. → 아이가 잘 _____ 제가 불을 껐어요. (different subjects; -도록 or -게) (Answer: 자도록|자게)
  3. ✗ 방이 따뜻하기 위해서 창문을 닫았어요. → 방이 _____ 창문을 닫았어요. (adjective, -게) (Answer: 따뜻하게)
  4. ✗ 시험에 붙으려고 같이 공부합시다. → 시험에 _____ 같이 공부합시다. (붙다, -기 위해서) (Answer: 붙기 위해서|붙기 위해)

Exercise 5 — Reading comprehension (새해 목표, 끝까지 지키는 법)

  1. 많은 사람들이 _____ 이 되기도 전에 포기해요. (which month?) (Answer: 2월|이월)
  2. 좋은 목표의 예: 일주일에 _____ 번 걷기 (how many, native Korean number) (Answer: 세)
  3. 목표를 잊어버리지 _____ 종이에 써서 붙이세요. (않다, as in the text) (Answer: 않도록)
  4. 목표를 _____ 이나 친구에게 말하세요. (who?) (Answer: 가족)
  5. 하루쯤 _____ 괜찮아요. (실패하다, as in the text) (Answer: 실패해도)

❓ Common Questions

Is -기 위해서 better than -(으)려고?

Not better — different in weight. -(으)려고 is the everyday way to explain a personal purpose (돈을 모으려고 아르바이트해요). -기 위해서 sounds a bit more deliberate and formal, suits bigger goals, appears constantly in writing, and can be followed by commands. In conversation, a mix of both sounds natural.

How do I say "a present for you" — 너를 위한 선물 or 너한테 줄 선물?

Both are fine. 너를 위한 선물 is "a present (made/chosen) for you", slightly warm and a little dramatic — you'll hear it in songs. 너한테 줄 선물 is the plain "a present I'm going to give you". In everyday speech the second is more common.

Can -도록 and -게 always replace each other?

In the "so that" meaning, almost always — choose -게 for chat and -도록 for anything formal or instructional. They separate in two places: the time/degree meaning (밤새도록, 밤늦도록) is -도록's, and -도록 하다 (polite instructions, resolutions) has no -게 equivalent with the same meaning, since -게 하다 means "make someone do".

Can I say -기로 했어요 about a group decision?

Yes, that's one of its main uses: 우리는 다음 달에 만나기로 했어요 ("We've arranged to meet next month"), 회사에서 휴가를 늘리기로 했대요 ("The company has decided to increase holidays, apparently"). -기로 하다 covers both personal decisions and agreements between people.

Challenge 2 (TOPIK-style): In 250–300 characters of 한다체, answer: 목표를 이루기 위해서 가장 중요한 것은 무엇인가? Use -기 위해서, -도록 and at least one contrast pattern from Lesson 7.

A goal about your own Korean is worth making specific too. "Get fluent" is a -(으)ㄹ까 하다 sort of goal; "read one short article a day for a month" is a -기로 했어요 goal. Plateaus at B1 usually come from vague goals, not a lack of talent — so set a small one, and keep it.
