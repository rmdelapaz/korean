Intermediate Korean — B1–B2, from TOPIK 3 to TOPIK 4

🌟 What This Course Covers

Twenty-six lessons take you from the end of A2 to a confident B2. You will pass on what people said, sound natural instead of textbook-correct, handle Korean offices, banks and workplaces, read news written in 한다체, and argue a point in speech and in writing.

Which level is this? Lessons 1–14 cover TOPIK level 3 (≈ CEFR B1) and lessons 15–26 cover TOPIK level 4 (≈ CEFR B2) — the grade 3–4 content of the National Institute of Korean Language's international standard curriculum, the stage language institutes call 3급–4급. Lesson 26 prepares you for TOPIK II.

Before you start: you should be comfortable with everything in the A1–A2 course — particles, -아요/어요 in every tense, honorific -(으)시-, irregular verbs, the basic connectors and noun modifiers. Your progress here is tracked separately.

🟠 B1 — Intermediate (TOPIK 3)

Lessons 1–14. Switch between speech styles, report speech, guess and infer, use the nuance endings, give reasons and conditions, link events in time — and handle life and work in Korea.
