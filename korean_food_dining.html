💡 Culture Note — 잘 먹겠습니다: Koreans say this before every meal. It literally means "I will eat well" and expresses gratitude to the person who prepared or paid for the food. After eating, say 잘 먹었습니다 ("I ate well"). It's considered polite and essential!

💬 Practice Dialogue

At a Korean Restaurant

Customer: 저기요! 비빔밥 하나하고 된장찌개 하나 주세요. (Excuse me! One bibimbap and one doenjang jjigae, please.)

Server: 네, 알겠습니다. 음료는요? (Yes, understood. Any drinks?)

Customer: 물 주세요. (Water, please.)

Server: 여기 있습니다. (Here you are.)

Customer: 잘 먹겠습니다! ... 아, 맛있어요! (I'll eat well! ... Ah, it's delicious!)

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • 주세요 (juseyo) = "please give me" — your most useful restaurant phrase
  • Say 잘 먹겠습니다 before eating, 잘 먹었습니다 after
  • 반찬 (side dishes) are free and refillable — just ask!
  • Call servers with 저기요! — it's normal and not rude in Korea
  • Korean food vocabulary: 밥 (rice), 국 (soup), 찌개 (stew), 고기 (meat), 면 (noodles)
