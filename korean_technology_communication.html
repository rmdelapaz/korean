💬 KakaoTalk (카카오톡)

KakaoTalk (카톡 for short) is THE messaging app in Korea — used by over 90% of the population. It's used for personal chat, group chats, business communication, payments (카카오페이), taxi hailing (카카오택시), and more.

⚠️ 여보세요 = Phone Only!

여보세요 is ONLY used when answering the phone. Do NOT use it as a face-to-face greeting — that would sound very strange. For in-person greetings, always use 안녕하세요.

✅ How ㅋㅋㅋ Works

More ㅋ's = more laughter. ㅋ (just acknowledged), ㅋㅋ (mild chuckle), ㅋㅋㅋ (laughing), ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ (really funny). It's the most common text response in Korean chat.

💡 Culture Note — Naver (네이버): Koreans use Naver (not Google) as their primary search engine. Naver is Korea's biggest portal — search, maps, news, blogs, shopping, webtoons, and even a dictionary. If you need to search something in Korea, try Naver first!

🎯 Summary

🎉 Key Takeaways

  • KakaoTalk (카톡) is essential — everyone in Korea uses it
  • 여보세요 is phone-only; never use it face-to-face
  • ㅋㅋㅋ = Korean "lol" — the most common texting response
  • Naver (네이버) is Korea's Google — search, maps, everything
  • Korea has some of the world's fastest internet — Wi-Fi is almost everywhere
